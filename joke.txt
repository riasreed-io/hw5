Here's what I don't understand about homework 5.

I asked for this one. That's the part that gets me. Nobody made me.
I walked up to a machine and I said: I need a new folder.

Five. There are five of them now. And don't ask me where 1 and 2
are, because they're not here, they were never here, we don't
discuss it.

And every single one of these folders does the identical job. It
holds things. Folders have held things flawlessly since the
beginning of recorded time. There has never once been a folder that
looked at a file and said, you know what, not today, I'm full.

So why a new one? What happened to the old one? Did it get TIRED?
Is there a folder somewhere with a bad back?

I had one drawer at my parents' house. Thirty years. Socks,
batteries, a passport. That drawer never got a sequel. That drawer
never needed a fresh start in a new location.

And now there's a joke.txt sitting in here, and the joke is about
the folder, and the only thing in the folder is the joke.

Pretty, pretty, pretty circular.
